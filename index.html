Featured Project

Autonomous Driving

NuScenes Pedestrian & Cyclist Motion Estimator

Built a robust sequence-to-sequence (seq2seq) motion forecasting model with attention for autonomous driving systems using the NuScenes dataset. The system predicts 6-second future trajectories (12 waypoints sampled at 2 Hz) for vulnerable road users (pedestrians and cyclists) under high scene uncertainty.

Tracking Accuracy: Achieved ~0.94m Average Displacement Error (ADE) on held-out test datasets.
State Estimation Support: Specially designed to assist downstream trajectory planners operating on noisy, occluded, or incomplete agent state estimates.
Robust Evaluation: Benchmarked for extreme occlusion scenarios and diverse weather conditions in urban layouts.
